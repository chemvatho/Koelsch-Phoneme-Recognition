# 4 · Phonological normalisation + Kölsch G2P

Bridges **orthography and IPA**. Two pieces:

- **`kolsch_g2p.py`** — rule-based grapheme→phoneme converter (Kölsch diphthongs
  `eï→ɛɪ`, uvular `/ʁ/`, `<z>→/t͡s/`, `<w>→/v/`, `<ch>` ich-/ach-laut split,
  word-final devoicing). Converts any word, OOV included.
- **`data/lexicon.csv`** — the pronunciation dictionary (`kolsch, ipa, frequency`),
  generated by the converter over the corpus vocabulary.

The notebook then applies the phonological rules (degemination, long vowels,
Dehnungs-h) and tokenises each IPA word into phonemes (`p h | p h`).

**Policy:** dictionary first, converter for OOV. **Input:** manifest `text`
(orthography). **Output:** `ipa_wordform` + `phonetic` columns → notebooks 5 & 7.
